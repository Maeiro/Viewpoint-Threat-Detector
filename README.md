# Viewpoint Threat Detector

Project Zomboid Build 42 mod that improves situational awareness with alerts for nearby Project A-Life NPCs, sprinters, and zombies approaching from behind. It does not require Project Viewpoint, Viewpoint Extended Support, or A-Life Stance Dots.

Hostile, Careful, Neutral, Friendly, and Allied NPC warnings can each be enabled independently. All stance alerts are disabled by default while the A-Life warning system is being redesigned; the separate close Hostile alarm and firearm identification are also off by default. If several enabled stances are nearby, alerts appear from highest to lowest priority—Hostile, Careful, Neutral, Friendly, then Allied—with a distinct color for each. When Project A-Life Jimmy is installed, its “Your people” and allied standing are recognized as Allied instead of being reduced to Friendly. Unknown factions continue to use the A-Life default stance of Hostile. The warning distance is configurable from 5 to 100 tiles, with a default of 40. Alerts report distance and direction, and the optional firearm warning identifies carriers within each enabled stance.

The mod warns about regular zombies out of sight behind the player using DayZ-style radial indicators; this is the only visual mode for zombie tracking. By default, only zombies on the player's floor with a clear path (not blocked by walls or closed doors) trigger this warning; low climbable fences do not block detection. Both filters are configurable. Balanced range is enabled by default and follows the vanilla perception distance: 3.5 tiles normally and 6.5 with Keen Hearing. Disable balanced range to set a custom distance from 5 to 100 tiles. The rear detection angle is adjustable from 30 to 360 degrees (180 degrees by default), and each indicator points toward the nearest zombie in its sector. Larger angles widen the detection area toward the sides and front. The radial display uses 18-degree circular arc indicators around a larger centered radius and groups detected zombies into up to three directions around the character by default. The indicators smoothly blend between configurable close- and far-range colors and pulse when the nearest zombie in a sector reaches the configurable critical blink distance (1.5 tiles by default). The Radial Indicator Appearance options independently adjust the indicators' circle radius, icon size, and close- and far-range colors for zombies and sprinters; defaults preserve the current screen-adaptive radius, appearance, and colors. Radial Indicator Limits lets players display 1 to 8 rear-zombie directions (3 by default) and sprinter directions (1 by default), with the nearest sprinter in each direction represented. Zombie detection works without Project A-Life; A-Life NPCs are excluded from the zombie count.

An optional setting (off by default) ignores fallen zombies in rear warnings. A-Life alert cards can be dragged with the mouse; their position is saved between sessions. Radial indicators stay centered on the screen.

Sprinters have their own all-direction radial indicators and dedicated alarm. Configure their distance, critical blink distance (3 tiles by default), same-floor and line-of-sight filters, fallen-zombie handling, sound cue, and volume independently from the other alerts. Their indicators pulse when a sprinter enters the critical distance and transition smoothly from magenta at close range to purple at the edge of the active range. Sprinter detection excludes A-Life NPCs.

Vehicle alert suppression is configurable independently for rear zombies, sprinters, and A-Life NPCs. All three are disabled while inside a vehicle by default; sprinter alarms follow the sprinter setting, and the close Hostile alarm follows the A-Life setting. Existing saves migrate the previous vehicle-alert setting to all three controls.

Immersive audio-only alerts can be enabled in Mod Options to hide all visual alerts and display previews while preserving threat detection and all configured alarm sounds.

Alarm sounds are enabled by default: the regular zombie warning uses heartbeat, close Hostile A-Life uses radar ping, and sprinters use a soft beep. Additional choices include sonar ping, short alarm, and ten space-themed cues. The siren has been removed. Each sound can be previewed in-game with the configured preview key; alert-preview buttons show temporary A-Life cards or zombie/sprinter radial indicators. The optional layout preview shows all five A-Life stances and both radial indicator types without requiring real threats. Only the A-Life cards can be dragged to save their HUD position. The preview can be enabled in Mod Options or toggled with an optional, unassigned-by-default key. Alarm volume is configurable from 0% to 200% and defaults to 150%. The regular alarm is limited to zombie warnings by default; A-Life alerts are silent by default, and the separate close Hostile A-Life alarm can be enabled independently. Sprinter alarms have separate sound, volume, and distance settings, independent of the visual warning range; close Hostile A-Life alarm distance is configurable independently of the regular NPC warning range. A new alarm stops the previous one so alert sounds do not overlap. No other mod is required. An alarm plays when a new warning type appears, not on every scan. Sound asset licenses and credits are included in `42/SOUND_CREDITS.txt`.

Choose how A-Life alert cards appear in Mod Options: Compact NeatUI, Full NeatUI, or Vanilla Mode. Vanilla Mode is selected by default and works without NeatUI_Framework, while the two NeatUI styles use its panel textures when available. NeatUI panel backgrounds use the native panel renderer and remain available after the A-Life alert style is changed. Compact NeatUI removes text labels and shows icons, counts, direction arrows, and tile distances (such as `68t`), keeping the normal text and icon scale while sizing each card to its contents. A-Life arrows are drawn as triangles, rotate continuously toward each threat, and use the stance colors: red for Hostile, yellow for Careful, white for Neutral, green for Friendly, and blue for Allied. When Viewpoint Compass is active, A-Life arrows use its camera heading; otherwise, they fall back to the character's facing direction. Nearby firearm carriers use the vanilla M9 Pistol icon.

Small icons identify A-Life NPC alerts.

## Alerts and configuration

Configure the alerts through the in-game Mod Options. Use the General section's reset button to restore this mod's options to their fresh-install defaults. Project A-Life is required for NPC stance and firearm-carrier alerts; rear zombie alerts work independently.
